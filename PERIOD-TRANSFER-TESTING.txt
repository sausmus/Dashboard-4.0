DASHBOARD 4.0 — CLASSROOM PERIOD TRANSFER / PARTICIPATION PRESERVATION

WHAT CHANGED
- Classroom roster refresh now recognizes the same Google student ID moving between Dashboard periods.
- Participation history is transferred to the new period across ALL participation terms.
- The old period's participation record is removed after the transfer so the history is not duplicated.
- If the destination period already has participation for that student, the two totals are combined rather than overwritten.
- Roster refresh summaries report a move instead of treating it only as an add/remove when the transfer can be identified.

EXAMPLE
Before:
  Period 2 — John Smith — Quarter 1: 4/5

Google Classroom move:
  John Smith moves from Period 2 to Period 5

After refreshing the affected Classroom rosters:
  Period 2 — John Smith no longer appears
  Period 5 — John Smith — Quarter 1: 4/5

The same transfer is applied to Quarter 1–4 and Fall/Spring Semester records.

RECOMMENDED TEST
1. Pick one test student currently in one mapped Classroom period.
2. Give that student participation points in Participation Tracker.
3. If desired, switch to another participation term and give the same student a different total there too.
4. Move the student to a different Classroom course/period.
5. In Dashboard Settings > Classes & Rosters, click Refresh All Classroom Rosters.
6. Confirm the refresh summary reports the student as moved and says participation was preserved.
7. Open Participation Tracker in the new period and verify the previous totals are present.
8. Confirm the student no longer appears in the old period roster.
9. Open Student Picker for the new period and confirm it reads the same preserved total.

REFRESH ORDER
The transfer detection is designed to work whether the old period or new period is refreshed first. Refresh All is still the simplest way to guarantee both rosters are current.

NO NEW GOOGLE OAUTH SCOPES ARE REQUIRED.
