DASHBOARD 4.0 — MILESTONES 4, 5 & 6
=====================================

WHAT CHANGED
------------
Milestone 4 — Settings UX
- Settings opens as five clean cards:
  Classes & Rosters
  Google Classroom
  Participation Grades
  School Calendar
  School Year
- Only the selected section opens.
- Google OAuth Client ID / Cloud setup is now inside "Advanced Google Setup".
- Cards show live summaries (active classes, mappings, participation assignment status, etc.).

Milestone 5 — Grade Sync Polish
- Participation Grades now shows:
  assignments ready
  last grade sync
  students last synced
  issues from the last sync
- Each assignment shows Draft/Published status badges.
- Per-class last sync records successful grades, missing submissions, and failed grade writes.
- Preview Grades now verifies Classroom submissions when connected and flags:
  Ready
  Assignment not ready
  Missing Classroom submission
  No Google student ID
  Could not check submission
- Grades remain DRAFT grades only. Nothing is returned/finalized automatically.

Milestone 6 — Smarter Roster Refresh
- Roster rows show last refresh time.
- Refresh detects changes by stable Google student ID:
  added students
  removed students
  renamed students
- Same Google ID + different display name is treated as a rename, not a new student.
- "Import All Mapped Rosters" becomes "Refresh All Mapped Rosters" after initial import.
- Refresh-all gives aggregate change counts.
- Existing participation remains tied to stable Google IDs.

FILES TO REPLACE
----------------
settings.html
shared/dashboard-data.js

The following are included for convenience and are unchanged from Milestone 3.1:
shared/classroom-service.js
participation-tracker.html
student-picker.html

NO NEW GOOGLE CLOUD SCOPE IS REQUIRED FOR MILESTONES 4-6.
Your existing Milestone 3.1 Classroom scopes are sufficient.

RECOMMENDED TEST
----------------
1. Upload settings.html and shared/dashboard-data.js.
2. Open Settings. Confirm only the five section cards appear initially.
3. Open each card and make sure its controls still work.
4. Connect Google Classroom.
5. Open Google Classroom and refresh one unchanged roster.
   Expected: "no roster changes."
6. If possible, test after a Classroom roster change.
   Expected: added/removed/renamed counts are shown.
7. Open Participation Grades and Preview Grades.
   Expected: each student shows a sync readiness status.
8. Push Draft Grades.
   Expected: overview cards update with last sync time, students synced, and any issues.
9. Verify Student Picker ↔ Participation still share points normally.

SAFETY
------
Roster refresh does not reset participation totals for existing Google IDs.
Dropped students leave the active roster but their historical participation data remains associated with their stable ID.
Grade sync still writes draftGrade only.
