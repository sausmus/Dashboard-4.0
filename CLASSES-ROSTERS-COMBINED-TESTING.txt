Dashboard 4.0 — Combined Classes & Rosters UX

WHAT CHANGED
- Google Classroom and Classes & Rosters are now one Settings card.
- Every class has a Roster Source selector:
    Google Classroom
    Manual Roster
- Google-backed classes show course mapping, student count, roster refresh status, and Refresh Roster.
- Manual classes show student count and an Edit Roster button.
- Class display name and visibility are edited directly on the same card.
- Participation Grade Sync includes only active Google Classroom-backed classes.
- Switching a class to Manual does not delete its saved Classroom mapping/assignment.
- Switching it back to Google Classroom restores the last imported Classroom roster; Refresh Roster then brings it current.

RECOMMENDED TEST
1. Open Settings. Confirm there are now four main cards.
2. Open Classes & Rosters.
3. Confirm your existing Classroom-imported periods automatically show Google Classroom as their roster source.
4. Confirm each period shows the correct mapped course and student count.
5. Refresh one Classroom roster and verify the normal added/removed/renamed summary still works.
6. Pick a test period and switch Roster Source to Manual Roster.
7. Use Edit Roster, add a fake test name, and Save Manual Roster.
8. Open Participation Tracker / Student Picker and confirm the manual roster appears normally.
9. Return to Settings > Participation Grades. Confirm that manual period is excluded from Classroom assignment/grade sync.
10. Switch the test period back to Google Classroom. Confirm the last imported Google roster returns, then click Refresh Roster.
11. Confirm existing participation points for Google students are preserved.
12. Confirm Participation Grade Preview / Push Draft Grades still works for the Google Classroom-backed classes.
